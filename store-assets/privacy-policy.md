# Privacy Policy — Free Video Downloader browser extension

Last updated: 2026-09-26

The extension does not collect, transmit to its developer, or store personal data.

When you choose **Download video with Free Video Downloader** from a video's context menu, the extension reads the video source URL and the page URL. It passes the selected link to the Free Video Downloader desktop application through the `free-video-downloader://` protocol. The desktop application contacts the relevant website to inspect or download the media. Those requests are made from your computer.

The extension does not use analytics, advertising, cookies, or remote code. Your browser and the websites you visit may process data under their own privacy policies. Download only material you are authorized to save.
